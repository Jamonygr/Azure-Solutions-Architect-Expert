# Full-exam path

This path covers all 49 measured skills once as a primary objective and reinforces them in the capstones. Complete the labs in sequence because later scenarios assume the decision and evidence habits established earlier.

| Identity, governance, monitoring | 01–07 | Logging, monitoring, identity, authorization, key management, governance | 350 |
| Data | 08–13 | Relational, semi-structured, unstructured, integration, analytics | 300 |
| Business continuity | 14–17 | Recovery, backup, high availability, data resilience | 200 |
| Infrastructure | 18–25 | Compute, application architecture, migration, networking | 400 |
| Capstones | 26–27 | Greenfield and hybrid synthesis | 0 |

## Per-lab rhythm

1. Read the scenario once without looking at candidates.
2. Mark mandatory requirements and measurable quality targets.
3. Predict which candidates are ineligible before viewing the matrix.
4. Complete the five checkpoints and retain only synthetic or sanitized evidence.
5. Answer all 50 learner questions without opening the answer key.
6. Record the score in the [assessment dashboard](../assessment-dashboard.md).
7. Review every missed question by objective and checkpoint, then redo that checkpoint.
8. Process the material change request and update the decision, not just the implementation.

## Readiness signals

A score is diagnostic, not proof of readiness. Look for convergence across three signals:

- at least 80% on a bank after a delay, with no repeated reasoning error;
- a concise explanation of why each rejected candidate loses under the stated requirements;
- independent evidence for both a desired state and the absence of a prohibited state.

Use the [objective map](../objective-map.md) to locate uncovered skills. Use the [study plan](../study-plan.md) to schedule spaced retrieval rather than repeating the same bank immediately.
